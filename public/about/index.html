About Kids Games 4u

Kids Games 4u is a free website of browser games for children. Our learning games cover the alphabet, numbers, shapes, English words, spelling, the solar system, telling the time, safety, good habits and memory.

There is nothing to install and no account to create. Games run on phones, tablets and computers, and progress is saved in your own browser.

How we choose what to build

We aim for short levels, clear goals and friendly feedback, so a child can play for a few minutes and feel successful. Each game page explains how to play, which skills it practises and how a parent or teacher can continue the learning away from the screen.
