Contact us

Parents, teachers and advertisers can reach us by email at sathishkumar.kanda@gmail.com. Kids Games 4u is run by one person, Sathish Kumar, so every message is read by the person who makes the games. We usually reply within a few days.

What you can write to us about

  • A game that is not working on your phone, tablet or computer
  • An idea for a new game, or a topic your child or class is learning
  • Mistakes in a game or article, such as a wrong spelling or fact
  • Questions about privacy or advertising

Useful details to include

  • The name of the game or page
  • The device and browser you were using
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen

Report an ad or a problem

If you see an advertisement that seems unsuitable for children, please tell us the page and what the ad showed. A screenshot helps if you have one.

A note for children

Please ask a parent, carer or teacher to write to us for you, and do not send us your name, address, school or photos.

Postal address

Our postal address is listed in our Privacy Policy.
