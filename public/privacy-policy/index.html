Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Kids Games 4u (kidsgames4u.com) is directed to children and families. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to information when someone uses the site.

The short version

  • No registration, accounts or profiles.
  • We do not ask children for names, email addresses, photos or locations to use the site.
  • Game progress stays in your browser and is not sent to us.
  • We do not use analytics or social media tracking tools.
  • Ads are requested with child protections on, so they are not personalised.

Information saved on your device

Games use your browser's local storage to remember stars, unlocked levels, sound settings and game choices. Some games let a player type a name, such as a hero name or a name for a certificate. Those names stay on the device and are not sent to us. Please encourage children to use a nickname.

You can erase this information at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Voices and sound

Spoken words use the speech feature of your own browser or device. The games do not use the microphone or camera.

Website hosting

Like all websites, ours must receive basic technical information to deliver pages. Our hosting provider, Netlify, may process details such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time of visit to run and secure the service. We do not use this information to identify children or build profiles.

Advertising

We use, or plan to use, Google AdSense to pay for the site. We tag ad requests for child treatment. Under this setting Google turns off personalised advertising and remarketing and applies its ad-serving protections for children. Ads are chosen from general signals such as the content of the page and approximate location.

Google may still process technical information, such as IP address and device or browser details, and may use cookies or similar technologies where permitted to deliver ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in some regions, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, may see a privacy message before ads are shown.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. This site is designed with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's data protections of the GDPR and UK GDPR in mind. If a child has sent us personal information, such as by email, a parent or guardian can contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Parents and guardians can contact us to ask what information we hold about their child, ask us to delete it, or refuse further collection. Because we do not run accounts, we normally hold nothing beyond messages people send us. Information handled by Google is covered by Google's own privacy tools and policies.

Links to other sites

Ads and a few links may lead to other websites with their own privacy practices. We encourage parents to supervise children online.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top.
