About Number Rocket: a free game for ages 3-7

Number Rocket is a free counting and early maths game for children. Kids blast off across the Moon, Mars and Saturn while they count, order numbers, compare which is bigger and add small numbers up to 20.

The trip starts with counting things a child can see, which builds the link between a number and a real amount. From there, levels move on to putting numbers in order, counting in twos, comparing two numbers and adding small groups together. The space theme gives every level a clear goal, and the gentle steps mean a child who is just starting to count can still enjoy early success before trying harder sums.

How to play

Put the numbers in order, tap the right answer, or add up the objects. The owl coach cheers every correct answer.

Controls

Play by touch on a phone or tablet, or with a mouse on a computer. Tap an answer, or move numbers into place when a level asks for an order. The speaker button controls sound, and the Levels button opens the map.

Skills kids practise

  • Counting objects
  • Number order 1 to 20
  • Skip counting by twos
  • Bigger and smaller numbers
  • Simple addition

For parents and teachers

Ask your child to count out loud and point at each object, which keeps one number matched to one thing. Try the same idea with spoons, toy cars or stairs at home. When adding, let fingers help. Using them is a normal stage and supports understanding instead of getting in its way.
