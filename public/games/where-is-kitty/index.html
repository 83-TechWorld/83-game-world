About Where Is Kitty?: a free game for ages 4-8

Where Is Kitty? teaches small English prepositions: in, on, by, up, off, out, to and at. Kitty jumps into boxes, onto boxes, out of boxes and walks to the house, so children see what each word means while a friendly voice reads every sentence.

Small position words can be hard to explain, but they become clear when a child sees them happen. Here each word is tied to a simple action with Kitty, a box or a house. Spoken sentences support children who are still learning to read, while dragging Kitty turns the meaning into something they do themselves. Fill-the-gap levels then bring the same words into short everyday sentences, which is a helpful step towards reading and writing.

How to play

Tap each word card to watch Kitty and hear what the word means. Drag Kitty to the right spot, watch Kitty move and pick the word, and fill the gap in everyday sentences.

Controls

Use touch or a mouse. Tap word cards to see and hear them, drag Kitty to the right place, and tap the word that fills each gap. The speaker button controls the voice and sound, and the Levels button opens the map.

Skills kids practise

  • Prepositions: in, on, by, up, off, out, to, at
  • Reading short sentences
  • Listening to English
  • Position words (where is it?)

For parents and teachers

Act the words out with a soft toy and a box: put it in, on and by the box, then ask your child to give you directions. Children learning English often mix up these little words, so short, playful repetition works better than explaining grammar rules.
