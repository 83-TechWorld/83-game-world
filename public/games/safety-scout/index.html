About Safety Scout: a free game for ages 5-10

Safety Scout teaches children everyday safety at school and outside. Kids decide if each situation is safe or unsafe and hear a short, friendly rule after every answer. They spot dangers on a busy street, put fire-drill steps in order, and finish with a stranger-safety and kindness round.

Safety lessons stick better when children practise making the decision themselves. Each round presents a familiar situation at school, on the playground, on the way somewhere or around people they do not know. A short rule after every answer explains why a choice is safe or unsafe, so mistakes become useful moments. Five levels lead to badges and a Junior Safety Inspector certificate, giving children a proud finish to work towards.

How to play

Listen to each situation, then tap SAFE or UNSAFE. You have three safety shields per level. From level 2 a timer starts after the question is read. Earn a badge for every level and collect all five to get your Junior Safety Inspector certificate.

Controls

Use touch or a mouse. Listen to each situation and tap SAFE or UNSAFE. From level 2 a timer begins after the question is read. Your name for the certificate is optional and stays on your device. The speaker button controls sound.

Skills kids practise

  • Classroom and hallway safety
  • Playground and lunchtime safety
  • Bus and road safety
  • Fire drills and field trips
  • Stranger safety, privacy and kindness

For parents and teachers

Use the game to begin a conversation, then adapt its rules to your family, school and neighbourhood. Agree which adults your child can go to for help, and practise what to do if they feel unsure. A game supports these lessons but does not replace real supervision or road practice.
