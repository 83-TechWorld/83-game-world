About Color Grid Slide: a free game for ages 5-12

Color Grid Slide is a sliding puzzle where colored caps must be sorted into matching columns: blue circles, yellow stars, red squares, green triangles and purple diamonds. A cap can only slide into an empty hole, so children plan routes, loop caps around each other and think a few moves ahead.

Sliding puzzles build spatial reasoning: children must picture a few moves ahead and work out an order, because a cap can only move into an empty hole. The game grows slowly from a 3 by 3 board with three colours to a 5 by 5 board with five, then adds stones that never move, one-way arrows and rainbow pads that change a cap's colour. Every level is made by scrambling a sorted board backwards with the real rules, so every puzzle can be solved.

How to play

Tap or swipe a cap that is next to an empty hole to slide it in. Get every cap into the column with its color and shape at the bottom. Finish within par moves, without hints, for three stars. Watch out for stones, one-way arrows and rainbow pads that change colors.

Controls

Tap a cap next to an empty hole to slide it in, or swipe it. Tap a cap in the same row or column as a hole to push the whole line. On a computer, the arrow keys work too. Undo takes back a move, Reset starts the level again, and Hint makes the next good cap glow.

Skills kids practise

  • Spatial reasoning and planning ahead
  • Colors and shapes
  • Patience and problem solving
  • Learning from mistakes with Undo
  • Counting moves

For parents and teachers

Encourage your child to say a plan out loud, such as “first I move the red one up, then the blue one across”. If they get stuck, Undo is a great teacher: going back a few moves shows how one choice changes the board. Short sessions work best, and the shape on every cap means the game works for colour-blind children too.
