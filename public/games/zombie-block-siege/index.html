🧟 ZOMBIE BLOCK SIEGE

Hide behind your brick wall, pop up to shoot, clear each zone and advance block by block. Every level ends with a BIG zombie that breathes fire. Zombies carry axes and guns!
A / D move · Hold S crouch & hide behind the wall · Release S stand up & Click to shoot (aim with mouse) · R reload · E rebuild wall (costs 🧱 bricks, earned from kills) · Tab / 1 / 2 swap gun ↔ pistol · P pause · Esc menu
Zombie bullets & fire are blocked by bricks only when you crouch behind them — standing leaves your head and chest exposed. Walls crumble, so rebuild between waves.
