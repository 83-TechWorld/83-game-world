About Stick Spy Ops: a free game for ages 8+

Stick Spy Ops is a free stick figure spy game with 20 missions. Sneak past guards, hide in cover and take down enemy agents. Give your hero and the enemies the names of your friends.

Each of the 20 missions asks players to think before they act. Moving carelessly can leave the hero exposed, while using cover and choosing the right moment makes a mission easier. Simple stick figures keep the look light, and the name options let players personalise their game. It is an action game rather than a learning game, so it is best suited to older children who are comfortable with cartoon combat.

How to play

Move with A and D or the arrow keys, jump with W or Space, crouch with S to hide in cover and aim with the mouse. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons and touch the screen to aim.

Controls

On a computer, move with A and D or the arrow keys, jump with W or Space, crouch with S and aim with the mouse. On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen buttons and touch the screen to aim.

Skills kids practise

  • Hand and eye coordination
  • Planning and patience
  • Timing and quick decisions

For parents and teachers

This game includes cartoon stick-figure shooting and is recommended for ages 8 and up. Please try it first and decide whether it suits your child. If you use the naming feature, encourage made-up names rather than real classmates, and talk about the difference between game action and real life.
