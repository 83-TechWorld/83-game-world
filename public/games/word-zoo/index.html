About Word Zoo: a free game for ages 4-8

Word Zoo is a free English vocabulary game for children. Kids match, sort, listen to and spell the names of fruits, vegetables, farm animals, wild animals and sea animals.

Children meet everyday English words in groups that make sense to them, from fruit and vegetables to farm, wild and sea animals. Matching links a word to its picture, sorting shows how things belong together, listening trains the ear, and spelling draws attention to the letters inside a word. That mix makes the game useful for early readers and for children who are learning English as an additional language.

How to play

Drag or tap a word, then tap the matching picture. In sorting levels, put each animal or food in its home. In spelling levels, tap the letters in order.

Controls

Use touch or a mouse. Drag or tap a word, then tap its picture. Sorting levels ask you to place each item in its home, and spelling levels ask you to tap letters in order. The speaker button controls spoken words and sound.

Skills kids practise

  • New English words
  • Fruits and vegetables
  • Domestic, wild and water animals
  • Sorting into groups
  • Spelling

For parents and teachers

Repeat new words together and use them in real life, perhaps naming fruit at the shop or animals in a story. Ask simple sorting questions such as “Does it live on a farm or in the sea?” Children learning English benefit from hearing a word many times, so replaying a level is worthwhile.
