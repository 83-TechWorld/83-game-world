About Memory Matrix: a free game for ages 4-10

Memory Matrix is a picture memory game that trains focus and visual memory. Kids look at a grid of numbered pictures, the pictures hide behind numbered doors, and they find where each one was. Later levels ask for two or three pictures in a row, which picture went missing, and which two pictures swapped places.

The game starts small and grows from 4 tiles to as many as 16, so children can build concentration step by step across 20 levels. Remembering where a picture sits is only the beginning. Later rounds ask players to recall a short sequence, notice which picture has gone or spot two that have swapped. Those changes keep the challenge fresh and encourage children to look carefully rather than guess quickly.

How to play

Look carefully while the timer counts down, or tap Ready when you have remembered. Then tap the number door where the picture was hiding. You have three shields per level. In missing levels pick the picture that disappeared; in swap levels tap the two pictures that changed places.

Controls

Use a finger or a mouse. Study the pictures until the timer ends, or tap Ready when you feel sure. Then tap the numbered door where the picture was hiding. The speaker button controls sound, and the Levels button opens the map.

Skills kids practise

  • Visual and spatial memory
  • Concentration
  • Numbers 1 to 16
  • Spotting differences
  • New words for animals, food, space and more

For parents and teachers

Teach a simple strategy: say what you see and where it is, such as “cat in the top corner”. Stories can help too, linking pictures together in order. Take breaks when attention fades, since memory games work best in short sessions when a child still feels fresh and interested.
