About Sky Blaster: Zombie Flight: a free game for ages 6+

Sky Blaster: Zombie Flight is a bright cartoon side-scrolling shooter. A kid pilot with a jetpack and aviator goggles flies through sunny clouds, sunset canyons and a starry night, blasting goofy zombies that fly with propeller hats, balloons and jetpacks. There is no gore: every zombie turns into green smoke and a little dancing ghost.

Every level adds two more flying zombies and makes their flight a little bouncier, so the challenge grows slowly over 15 levels and three skies. The power stars float right beside the zombies, which rewards brave but careful flying: get close enough to grab them without bumping into anyone. A combo multiplier, screen shake on big hits and a Level Up banner give the game an arcade feel, while the cartoon style keeps it light.

How to play

Drag to fly, or use the arrow keys or WASD. Your blaster fires by itself, or turn auto-fire off and shoot with Space. Swoop close to the zombies to grab the glowing power stars: they upgrade your blaster from Pea-Shooter to Twin Laser to Super Mega-Blast. Flying close without touching gives a Close! bonus that charges your shield. Each level adds more zombies, Tank Zombies arrive from level 2 and a Mega-Zombie boss waits at levels 5, 10 and 15.

Controls

On a phone or tablet, drag your finger to fly; the pilot flies just ahead of your finger so you can always see it. Tap the 💨 button for a barrel-roll dash that makes you safe for a moment. On a computer, use the arrow keys or WASD, or drag with the mouse. Space shoots when auto-fire is off, Shift dashes and P pauses.

Skills kids practise

  • Hand and eye coordination
  • Quick decisions and risk versus reward
  • Spatial awareness while dodging

For parents and teachers

This is a cartoon action game with blasters, recommended for ages 6 and up. Defeated zombies become little dancing ghosts and there is no blood or gore. For younger players, turn on Little Pilot mode for five hearts and slower zombies, and leave auto-fire on so they can focus on flying.
