About Alphabet Safari: a free game for ages 3-6

Alphabet Safari is a free ABC game that helps preschool and kindergarten children learn the English alphabet. Kids travel through jungle, ocean, space, candy and dinosaur worlds, putting letters in order, finding missing letters and matching sounds.

Each world turns a small alphabet skill into a short challenge, so children get plenty of practice without long lessons. Early levels help a child recognise letters and see how the alphabet runs from A to Z. Later levels look at capitals and small letters, vowels and the sounds letters make. Because every level is brief, it suits a few minutes before school or a quiet moment after dinner, and children can replay a favourite world whenever they like.

How to play

Drag a letter into its box, or tap the letter and then tap the box. Finish each level to earn up to three stars and unlock the next world.

Controls

Works with a finger on a phone or tablet and with a mouse on a computer. Drag a letter, or tap it and then tap its box. The speaker button turns sound on or off, and the Levels button returns to the map.

Skills kids practise

  • Recognising letters A to Z
  • Alphabetical order
  • Uppercase and lowercase letters
  • Vowels and consonants
  • Letter sounds (phonics)

For parents and teachers

Say each letter sound together rather than only the letter name, because sounds help most with early reading. Away from the screen, hunt for the same letters on cereal boxes, street signs and picture books. If a level feels hard, replay an easier one first. Confidence matters more than speed at this age.
