About Shape Dash: a free game for ages 3-8

Shape Dash is a free shapes and colours game for toddlers and young children. Kids steer circles, squares, triangles and stars into the matching hole while dodging buzzy bees.

Moving a shape to its matching hole asks a child to name what they see and compare it with the targets around them. Buzzy bees add a little excitement and encourage careful steering, so young players practise control as well as recognition. Circles, squares, triangles and stars are familiar first shapes, and seeing them again in different colours helps children understand that a shape stays the same even when its colour changes.

How to play

Use the arrow buttons or keys, or press and drag, to move the shape to the hole with the same shape.

Controls

On a computer, use the arrow keys or press and drag. On a phone or tablet, use the arrow buttons or drag with a finger. Steer around the bees and into the hole with the same shape. The speaker button controls sound.

Skills kids practise

  • Naming basic shapes
  • Matching shapes and colours
  • Hand and eye control
  • Quick thinking

For parents and teachers

Name each shape together and talk about its sides and corners: a triangle has three, a square has four, and a circle has none. Then go on a shape hunt around your home. Very young children may enjoy steering while you help watch for bees, which makes it a good game to share.
