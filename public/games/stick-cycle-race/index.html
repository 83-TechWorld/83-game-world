About Stick Cycle Race: a free game for ages 6+

Stick Cycle Race is a free 20-level cycling game. Choose your colour, pedal fast, grab boosters and try to beat the other riders and the ghost.

Racing is about more than holding a button. Players choose when to change lanes, when to collect boosters and when to spend stamina on a sprint, all while rival riders and the ghost keep up the pressure. Twenty levels provide a steady climb in challenge, and choosing a colour makes the rider feel personal. Short races work well for quick play and encourage children to try again and improve.

How to play

Hold the pedal button (or Space or D) to go, press W and S to change lane, and hold Shift to sprint when you have stamina.

Controls

Hold the pedal button, Space or D to ride. Press W and S to change lanes, and hold Shift to sprint when you have stamina. On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen buttons.

Skills kids practise

  • Timing and reaction speed
  • Managing stamina
  • Persistence and improving with practice

For parents and teachers

This racing game is recommended for ages 6 and up and has no fighting. It can be a good moment to talk about trying again after losing. If your child enjoys cycling in real life, remind them that helmets, looking both ways and safe places to ride matter outside the game.
