About Habit Hero: a free game for ages 4-9

Habit Hero follows a child's whole day, from waking up at sunrise to sleeping under the stars. Kids pick the good habit from two picture cards, brush germs off a tooth, fill a healthy plate, wash hands step by step, tidy their toys and pack their school bag. Every good habit fills the Energy, Clean and Smile meters.

Instead of treating habits as a list of rules, the game walks through one full day so each choice appears at a natural moment. Children compare two picture cards, then try hands-on activities such as brushing germs away, building a healthy plate, washing hands in order, tidying toys and packing a school bag. Meters for Energy, Clean and Smile show how small good choices add up, with a badge for each part of the day.

How to play

Listen to both cards, then tap the good habit. Play the mini-games: tap the germs, drag food to the right basket, tap the steps in order, drag toys into the toy box and pack the right things in your bag. Earn a badge for every part of the day.

Controls

Use touch or a mouse. Listen to the cards and tap the good habit. In mini-games, tap germs, drag food and toys into place, and tap steps in order. The speaker button controls sound, and the Levels button opens the map.

Skills kids practise

  • Morning and bedtime routines
  • Hygiene: brushing, bathing, handwashing
  • Healthy food choices
  • Good manners and sharing
  • Tidiness and getting ready for school

For parents and teachers

Connect each level to your own routine by asking, “What do we do next in the morning?” A simple picture chart at home can build on the game. Notice effort and celebrate one habit at a time, because steady encouragement usually works better than correcting everything at once.
