# Shorts and Reels: ready-to-post text

Each video is 20 seconds, 1080×1920, with captions burned in. Each one has a full soundtrack:
- **Voiceover:** generated with Kokoro, an open voice model licensed for commercial use.
- **Music:** original background music composed in code for these videos, so there are no third-party music rights to manage.
- **Sound effects:** pops and chimes timed to the real taps in the gameplay.

If you add a trending sound in the Instagram app instead, turn the original audio down rather than off, so the voiceover still plays.

Pick the cover frame at about 2 seconds, once the hook text is fully on screen.

Check every hashtag's post count in Instagram search before posting. The aim is 2–3 small tags (under 50k posts), 1 medium tag and at most 1 large tag. Rotate the sets between posts rather than reusing one block.

## Before you post

- **Instagram link:** links in captions can't be tapped, so put kidsgames4u.com in your bio.
- **YouTube audience setting:** YouTube asks whether each video is "made for kids". These are aimed at parents, but the setting is your legal choice. Videos marked as made for kids lose comments and some features.
- **Posting rhythm:** post one video every 2 or 3 days rather than all four at once, so each one gets its own push.
